The Fall of Robespierre
was published in 1794 by
Samuel Taylor Coleridge and Robert Southey.

by
C. B. Bowman,

Jonathan Ingram, Lisa Reigel, Michael Zeug, and Distributed Proofreaders

Internet Archive.

The Festival of the Fédération, 14 July 1790, on the Champ-de-Mars,
a painting completed in 1792 by
Charles Thévenin.

League Spartan and Sorts Mill Goudy
